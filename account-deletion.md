# NextTARGET – Account Deletion Request

## How to Request Account Deletion

NextTARGET users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

To request deletion:

1. Send an email to **nobinsutradhar5@gmail.com**.
2. Set the subject to **NextTARGET Account Deletion Request**.
3. Include the email address registered with your NextTARGET account.
4. We will verify your request and process it.

## What Data Is Deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed, the following associated information will be deleted:

- Registered email address
- Username
- Personal goals
- Accountability tasks

## How Long Does Deletion Take?

We will review and process account deletion requests within a reasonable period. If additional verification is required, we may contact you using the email address provided.
