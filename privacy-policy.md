# NextTARGET Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

NextTARGET ("we", "our", or "the app") is a productivity and personal accountability application designed to help users manage their goals, track daily tasks, and improve consistency.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and how users can request deletion of their data.

By using NextTARGET, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

NextTARGET collects and stores the following information when you create and use an account:

- **Email address:** Used for account registration and authentication.
- **Username:** Used to identify your account within the application.
- **Goals:** Goals you create and manage in the application.
- **Accountability tasks:** Tasks and related information you create to track your daily progress.

We do not intentionally collect additional personal information beyond what is necessary for these features.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Authenticate users and provide account access.
- Save and display personal goals.
- Save and manage accountability tasks.
- Provide the application's core productivity and tracking features.
- Maintain and improve the functionality of NextTARGET.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

NextTARGET uses **Supabase** as its backend service for account authentication and data storage.

The information described in this policy is processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Supabase may process information on our behalf to provide authentication, database, hosting, and related backend services. Its processing is subject to its own privacy practices.

For more information, please review the [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase when necessary to operate the application.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to comply with valid legal requests.

## 6. Data Security

We rely on the security features and infrastructure provided by our backend service, Supabase, and take reasonable measures to protect stored information.

However, no internet-based service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

To request account deletion:

1. Send an email to **nobinsutradhar5@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line "NextTARGET Account Deletion Request".
3. Include the email address associated with your NextTARGET account.

We will review the request and process the deletion.

Users can also contact us through the NextTARGET Community WhatsApp group for questions or assistance. The community is an external communication platform and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

## 8. Children's Privacy

NextTARGET is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published at this policy's public URL, with the revised date shown at the top.

If future versions introduce additional features, such as subscriptions or payment processing, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices before those features are released.
